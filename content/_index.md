Bitcoin Reseacher & developer contributing full-time to open-source Bitcoin projects. My
main stack is **Rust**.

I work on Bitcoin privacy. Most of my time goes to
[Payjoin](https://payjoin.org), through the
[rust-payjoin](https://github.com/payjoin/rust-payjoin) implementation, where
I've contributed `no_std` support for embedded targets, fuzzing infrastructure,
FFI bindings, and review.

I'm a fellow at [Vinteum](https://vinteum.org), a non-profit research and
development center for Bitcoin in Brazil.

I learn and build in the open. The [blog](/posts/) is mostly notes turned into
structured explanations, protocol walkthroughs, logbooks, and whatever I'm
currently studying.
